## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT**. This trainer provides quality-of-life controls for the text-based adventure experience, including adjustable reading speed, pause control, and convenient management of choice-driven story progression. It is designed for Steam and does not add combat, free-roaming movement, or inventory mechanics that are not part of the game.

**Target Audience:** Players who want faster text review, easier route testing, more convenient phone-trigger experimentation, or a smoother replay experience in the single-player visual novel.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT does not use free-roaming character movement. Progress is handled through dialogue, menus, scene transitions, and phone interactions. Use the trainer's pause and text-speed controls while navigating these story screens.

### Tools & Inventory

The game has no conventional inventory or equipment system. Important progression is tied to dialogue choices, phone calls, emails, replies, and ignored messages rather than collectible items or character gear.

### World & Environment

Akihabara serves as the primary narrative setting and visual backdrop. The game presents locations through illustrated scenes, written narration, conversations, and branching events instead of an explorable open world.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat system, battle interface, health meter, or weapon progression in STEINS;GATE RE:BOOT. The trainer therefore focuses on narrative pacing, phone-trigger decisions, and route management rather than combat advantages.
